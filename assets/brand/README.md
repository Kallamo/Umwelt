# Umwelt brand

The Umwelt mark is a lowercase m over its own reflection. Drawn with the same
stroke, a w is an m turned 180 degrees: the same form, seen from the other side.
For Jakob von Uexküll, an Umwelt closes on two sides, the Merkwelt (what a
being perceives) and the Wirkwelt (what it does), and both sit in the middle of
the name: u**MW**elt. The m is ink; the w carries the spectrum shared with
Kallamo.

## Files

| `umwelt-signature-on-dark.svg`, `umwelt-signature-on-light.svg` | The lockup signed "by Kallamo", for pages that present Umwelt on its own. |
| `umwelt-mark-on-dark.svg`, `umwelt-mark-on-light.svg` | The mark alone, from 48 px up. |
| `umwelt-mark-small-on-dark.svg`, `umwelt-mark-small-on-light.svg` | The mark below 48 px: heavier stroke, wider gap. |

| `favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png` | Browser tabs. The SVG switches ink with the color scheme. |
| `icon-128.png`, `icon-512.png` | Package and avatar icons, on the dark ground. `assets/icon.png` is a copy of `icon-128.png` for the NuGet package. |

| `tokens.css`, `tokens.json` | Colors and construction values. |

"on-dark" files are for dark grounds (white ink); "on-light" files are for
light grounds (ink `#1a202c`). The spectrum is the same on both.

## Construction

- One stroke (11 on a 120 grid) and one radius (20) for the mark and every
  letter of the logotype.
- The w is the m turned 180 degrees, around the centre of the mark.
- A gap of 6 between the m and the w: the horizon.
- Below each arch, a straight stem of 8: the mark is wider than it is tall, and
  in the lockup it stays within the height of the word.
- Round forms overshoot the flat ones by 1.2; visible stem ends have a 1.6
  corner radius.
- Below 48 px, use the small mark (stroke 15, stem 6, horizon 10). The 16 px
  favicon opens the horizon to 15 so it stays visible.

## Do not

- Recolor the mark, put the spectrum on the m, or change the order of its
  colors.
- Stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to any file.
- Set "umwelt" in a typeface; use the logotype file.
- Place the on-dark files on light grounds, or the reverse.

"Umwelt" and "Kallamo" are trademarks of their creator. "by Kallamo" in the
signature is Inter Medium, outlined, so it renders the same everywhere.
