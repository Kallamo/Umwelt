     commercial product, service or brand (an advergame). Showing the
     authors' own skills is not such promotion (see section 2).

## 2. What is not Monetization

These do not count as Monetization, as long as no payment unlocks any content,
feature, advantage or early access for whoever pays:

- **Donations**: voluntary donations to the authors, for example through
  Patreon or Ko-fi.
- **Pay what you want**: a store page that suggests a payment but lets every
  Player download the same Play Work for free, with a minimum price of zero
  (for example "name your own price" on itch.io). Any payment made there is a
  donation.
- **Crowdfunding**: a campaign that funds the making of a Play Work that will
  be free, when its rewards are only thanks, the backer's name in the credits,
  or goods outside the Play Work, such as merchandise or an art book. Rewards
  that give access to the Play Work or any part of it before or beyond other
  Players (early or beta access, exclusive content, in-game items), or that
  put content created or chosen by the backer into the Play Work, are
  Monetization.
- **Distribution platforms**: advertising that a store, mod site or other
  platform shows on its own pages, and reward programs through which a
  platform pays authors without charging Players.
- **A free mod for a paid game**: the mod is the Play Work, and it must be
  free; the game it modifies may be sold by others.
- **Recognition and opportunities**: using the Play Work in a portfolio, to
  seek employment or commissions, for academic credit, or to enter a game jam
  or competition, including one that awards prizes, and receiving a grant or
  award for having made it, as long as Players are never charged.

## 3. The permission

If you convey a Play Work that includes, links to, embeds or communicates with
Umwelt, and you meet the conditions in section 4, then:

1. you are not required to license the rest of the Play Work under the AGPL,
   or to provide its Corresponding Source, including under section 13 of the
   AGPL for a server that runs a game for its Players; you may distribute it
   under terms of your choice;
2. for unmodified Umwelt, you meet the AGPL's requirement to provide its
   Corresponding Source by stating which version of Umwelt you use and
   linking to its official source at https://github.com/Kallamo/Umwelt.

Umwelt itself stays under the AGPL with this exception. If you modify Umwelt,
your modified Umwelt is covered by the AGPL, and you must make its
Corresponding Source available as the AGPL requires. Only the rest of the Play
Work is released from the AGPL.

## 5. When the conditions stop being met

If a Play Work stops meeting the conditions, for example because it starts
Monetization, this permission ends for that Play Work 30 days later. Within
those 30 days you may:

- bring the Play Work back within the conditions;
- release the Play Work under the AGPL;
- obtain a commercial license from the Copyright Holder; or
- stop distributing versions of the Play Work that use Umwelt.

Copies conveyed to Players while the conditions were met remain permitted.
