# Kallamo Code of Conduct

Kallamo builds tools for people who tell stories: writers, game makers,
tabletop players and the developers who work with them. This code of conduct
describes how we treat each other while we do it.

It applies to every Kallamo project, including Umwelt and the Kallamo app, and
to every space where those projects happen: repositories, issues, pull
requests and discussions, the Kallamo Discord, events, and any conversation in
which someone represents Kallamo or one of its projects.

## Our commitment

We want everyone who takes part to be able to do so without harassment,
whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, religion, level of experience, education,
social or economic background, appearance, or the language they speak best.

## What we expect

- Be kind, and assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Disagree with ideas, never with people. Explain why, and be open to being
  wrong.
- Welcome newcomers. Every question was once asked by someone who is now an
  expert; answer it as you would have liked to be answered.
- Give feedback that helps: specific, about the work, and with a way forward.
  Receive it the same way.
- Respect people's time, boundaries and names, including the pronouns they
  ask you to use.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, apologize to those
  affected, and learn from it.
- Keep in mind that we work across languages and cultures. Write plainly and
  read generously.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including continued
  contact after someone has asked you to stop.
- Insults, slurs, demeaning jokes and personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery directed at someone, and unwelcome sexual
  attention.
- Threats of violence, or encouraging someone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, an e-mail or
  a real name they have not shared, without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, events or work.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

Stories deal with dark themes, and the worlds people build with our tools may
contain violence, crime or cruelty. Discussing that fiction is welcome.
Using fiction as a cover to target a real person is not.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this code, write to
**conduct@kallamo.com**, or send a direct message to a moderator on the Kallamo
Discord. Include what happened, where and when, and links or screenshots if
you have them. You do not need to be sure it is a violation to report it.

Every report is read, answered and handled in confidence. We share the
reporter's identity only with their consent. If a report concerns someone who
would normally handle it, they take no part in handling it.

Reporting in good faith is never a reason for any action against the person
who reported.

## Enforcement

The maintainers of Kallamo projects are responsible for enforcing this code.
They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow it. Depending on what happened and whether
it keeps happening, they may:

1. **Correct.** A private note explaining what was wrong, and sometimes a
   request for a public apology.
2. **Warn.** A warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a period
   with no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Suspend.** A temporary ban from interacting with the community.
4. **Ban.** A permanent ban from all Kallamo community spaces.

Serious violations, such as threats or sustained harassment, may lead directly
to a ban. A person who was suspended or banned may ask for the decision to be
reviewed by writing to the address above.

## Changes

This code may be updated over time. The version in the repository of each
Kallamo project is the one that applies to it.
